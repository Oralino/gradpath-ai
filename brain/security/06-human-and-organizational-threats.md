# 06 Human And Organizational Threats

> Defensive cybersecurity reference. Test only systems you own or are explicitly authorized to assess.

## 7. Human and organizational threats

### 7.1 Phishing

**What it is:** Deceptive communication attempts to obtain credentials,
sensitive information, or unsafe actions.

**Defenses:** - Use MFA or passkeys. - Verify unusual requests through a
separate trusted channel. - Train users to recognize suspicious links
and attachments. - Provide a simple reporting process. - Use email
authentication controls such as SPF, DKIM, and DMARC where applicable.

### 7.2 Social engineering

**What it is:** Manipulation of people into disclosing information or
bypassing procedures.

**Defenses:** - Verify identity before sensitive account or access
changes. - Use approval and separation-of-duties processes. - Avoid
sharing secrets through chat or email. - Train staff and document
escalation procedures.

### 7.3 Insider threats

**What they are:** People with legitimate access misuse it intentionally
or accidentally.

**Defenses:** - Apply least privilege and periodic access reviews. -
Separate sensitive duties. - Log and review privileged actions. - Revoke
access promptly when roles change. - Use data-loss prevention controls
where appropriate. - Establish clear policies and reporting channels.

### 7.4 Ransomware

**What it is:** Malware or an attacker makes systems or data
unavailable, often while demanding payment.

**Defenses:** - Maintain offline or immutable backups. - Patch operating
systems and applications. - Use endpoint protection and least
privilege. - Segment networks. - Restrict macros and untrusted
executables where appropriate. - Practice recovery exercises and
incident response.
